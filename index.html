02 · How he works

One mole. Four jobs.

Every $MOLERO buy and sell pays a 2% creator fee. The verified XMR ROOTS program splits it, and the mole carries every piece to where it belongs.

2%

Collect

He pops up on the Solana surface, grabs the creator fees from trading and drags them down into the coin's own program vault.

40%

Deliver

Holder mode. Snapshots are taken, and the mole tunnels wXMR straight to every eligible wallet holding at least $20. You don't need to claim anything.

35%

Feed the roots

Deepens the shared ROOTS/wXMR liquidity pool. The new LP tokens get burned, so those roots never get pulled.

25%

Bury

Buys back ROOTS and buries it forever. Every burn is a real Solana transaction you can check.
